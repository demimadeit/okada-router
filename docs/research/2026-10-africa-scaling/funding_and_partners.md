## Key Question 1: Africa Deep Tech Challenge (ADTC) 2026 — organiser, what happened after Gate 1, Gate 2/3 dates and criteria, finalists, prizes, post-challenge support

### Takeaway
ADTC 2026 ("The Laptop LLM Challenge") is already over: 20 semifinalists were named, 10 finalists were announced (Devpost says 29 Sep; the ADTC Substack post is dated 7 Oct), the live pitch final was held 17 Oct 2026 and results were published 19 Oct 2026 (1st Agritgllm/Togo, 2nd Kuza AI, 3rd Muta). Okada Router does not appear in the published 10-finalist list; whether it made the 20-semifinalist list could not be verified because the semifinalist post could not be fetched. The useful residual value is (a) the organiser's network (Future Africa, Accelerate Africa, AI Africa XPrize, Udu Technologies/Africa GPU Hub, IHS) and (b) the 2027 edition, which is recurring (2025 and 2026 editions both ran).
