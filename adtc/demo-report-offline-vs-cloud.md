**Dropping the First Collection:**

**Positioning statement for the drop:**
- Introduce the six numbered compositions as six canvases, each with its own unique story and sentiment.
- Position the CANVAS collection as the pinnacle of the brand, a reflection of the highest artistic expression within the realm of perfumery.

**Drop format and timing:**
- Launch in waves over the next three months.
- The first wave will include CANVAS I to IV, and ETERNALS.
- ETERNALS will be released as a limited edition, ensuring exclusivity and maintaining the scarcity.
- Sample sets of 5ml will be available at the launch, encouraging early adopters to experience the product.

**Scarcity and pricing structure:**
- The 5ml sample sets will be priced at $50, offering an initial taste of the product.
- Sample sets will be sold exclusively through the brand’s website and select retailers.
- The full CANVAS and ETERNALS collections will be priced at $150 per bottle, with a 10% discount for the first 100 units sold.

**Pre-launch: How to build the waitlist:**
- Utilize social media, influencers, and email newsletters to build anticipation and encourage early engagement.
- Offer exclusive access to early viewing of product photography and create a sense of exclusivity.
- Promote the limited edition ETERNALS as a must-have item for those who want to experience the brand’s highest artistic achievement.

**Launch week: Day-by-day plan:**
- **Day 1-3:** Introduce the brand’s philosophy and the importance of the CANVAS collection, emphasizing the brand’s stance on fine art over cosmetics.
- **Day 4:** Release the sample sets for the first wave of CANVAS and ETERNALS.
- **Day 5-7:** Highlight the limited edition ETERNALS and its exclusivity, and encourage early buyers to experience the product.
- **Day 8-10:** Launch the first full bottles of CANVAS and ETERNALS, focusing on the experience of the product and the brand’s artistic vision.

**Content and imagery direction:**
- Promote the gallery-like aesthetic with imagery that evokes a sense of refinement and contemplation.
- Use rich, neutral tones and gallery lighting to create a warm and intimate atmosphere.
- Incorporate poetry in the copy to enhance the artistic and poetic quality of the products.

**Three risks and how to avoid them:**
1. **Risk:** Lack of product variety or limited availability.
   - **Mitigation:** Ensure the product variety is as rich and varied as the brand’s artistic vision.
2. **Risk:** Inadequate pre-launch marketing strategy.
   - **Mitigation:** Use a multi-channel approach, including social media, influencers, and email newsletters to build anticipation.
3. **Risk:** Poor content and imagery direction.
   - **Mitigation:** Ensure the content and imagery align with the brand’s gallery aesthetic and artistic vision, focusing on refined and contemplative imagery.

### 1. Positioning Statement  
- **The Last Avenue presents perfume as fine‑art, inviting collectors to step into a curated gallery where each scent is a brushstroke on a shared canvas.**  
- **Canvas I‑VI are limited‑edition “exhibits” that transform personal fragrance rituals into a contemplative, tactile experience.**  

---

### 2. Drop Format & Timing  

| Option | Rationale | Recommendation |
|--------|-----------|----------------|
| **All six scents launched simultaneously** | • Immediate “gallery opening” impact.<br>• Reinforces the “Six canvases. One gallery.” narrative.<br>• Simplifies logistics and messaging. | **Go all‑in on Day 1** – the concept hinges on the collective set, not on staggered discovery. |
| Wave release (2‑3 scents per week) | • Extends media life.<br>• Risks diluting the unified artistic statement. | **Reject** – the brand’s core promise is a complete curated collection. |

**Timing:**  
- **Pre‑launch window:** 4 weeks (waitlist building).  
- **Launch week:** 7 days, with a single “Opening Night” drop on Day 1, followed by supporting activations.  

---

### 3. Scarcity & Pricing Structure  

- **Full‑size Eau de Parfum (50 ml):** Premium price tier – **$280** (reflects artisan, limited‑edition status).  
- **5 ml Sample Set (Canvas Sampler):** Introductory tier – **$45** (covers production, encourages trial, builds perceived rarity).  
- **Scarcity cues:**  
  - Limited run of **300 bottles per composition** (hard‑cap disclosed on product page).  
  - “Only 5 ml samples left” badge on waitlist confirmation email.  
- **Bundling:** Offer a **“Gallery Pass”** – purchase any full‑size and receive the 5 ml set at **30 % off** (adds value, drives upsell).  

---
